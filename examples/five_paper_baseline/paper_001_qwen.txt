This study evaluated the clinical effectiveness of an AI-based non-invasive embryo selection system and developed predictive models for IVF outcomes. Using data from 300 IVF cycles, the study found that higher AI-derived embryo scores were significantly associated with improved reproductive outcomes, including implantation, clinical pregnancy, ongoing pregnancy, and live birth rates. The AI model demonstrated excellent performance with an accuracy of 84.3%, sensitivity of 82.1%, specificity of 79.8%, and an AUC-ROC of 0.89. Multivariable logistic regression identified maternal age, AMH levels, high-quality blastocysts, and AI scores ≥85 as independent predictors of clinical pregnancy. While the AI model showed strong predictive capability, its performance was based on data from a single center, limiting generalizability. The study highlights the potential of AI to enhance embryo selection accuracy and support personalized reproductive care, but further research is needed to validate these findings in diverse clinical settings.
