This study presents a lightweight and robust image steganography method, ISA-ELCN, designed for secure communication. The method integrates improved Inception-V3 and Xception convolutional blocks with a RepVGG reparameterization mechanism to reduce computational complexity while maintaining feature representation. A dense connection mechanism in the decoding network enhances feature reuse, and a discriminant network applies adversarial constraints to improve security and anti-detection capabilities. Experimental results show that ISA-ELCN achieves a peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) of 40.7 dB, structural similarity index (SSIM) of 0.969, and learned perceptual image patch similarity (LPIPS) of 0.055 on the test set, outperforming comparative methods. The algorithm maintains a bit error rate (BER) of 0.008 and a recovery accuracy (RA) of 0.82 under 30 compression, and RA of 0.98 with BER of 0.23 under 90 compression. It also demonstrates strong robustness under noise interference, with RA of 0.96 and BER of 0.22 at a noise standard deviation of 0.05. The method balances performance and efficiency, offering a practical solution for secure image steganography. Limitations include reliance on GPU resources and limited real-world distortion testing.
