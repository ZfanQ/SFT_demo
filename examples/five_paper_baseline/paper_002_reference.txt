This study proposes ISA-ELCN, an image steganography method designed to balance concealment, secret-image recovery, robustness, and computational efficiency. Its embedding-extraction architecture combines improved Inception-V3 and Xception modules, RepVGG reparameterization, dense decoder connections, and adversarial discrimination. Evaluation on BOSSBase 1.01 reported test-image PSNR of 40.7 dB, structural similarity of 0.969, and a bit error rate of 0.008. The method also outperformed the evaluated baselines under compression and noise, with additional tests covering scaling, cropping, and ImageNet and COCO subsets. Compared with SRIS-Net, model parameters decreased from 8.72 million to 5.46 million, and inference time decreased from 19.50 to 15.10 milliseconds in the reported environment. The results support an improved performance-efficiency trade-off under the tested conditions. Limitations include predominantly grayscale evaluation, incomplete coverage of combined real-world distortions, and continued reliance on GPU resources during training.
