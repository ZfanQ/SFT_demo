This study examined the role of lncRNA PGM5P4-AS1 in breast cancer progression, finding it significantly downregulated in tumor tissues and associated with poor prognosis. Functional experiments showed PGM5P4-AS1 suppresses cancer cell growth by sponging miR-3664-5p and modulating KLF9. However, the study's retrospective design, small sample size, and lack of in vivo validation limit its conclusions.
