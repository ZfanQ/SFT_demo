Oncolytic virus OVV-03 enhances CAR-T cell therapy against glioblastoma by modulating immune checkpoints and upregulating HER2, improving T cell infiltration and cytotoxicity. In vitro and in vivo studies showed significant tumor suppression and prolonged survival, though results are based on female murine models and require human validation. The study highlights OVV-03's potential as an immunovirotherapy platform but notes limitations in clinical translation.
