Proteinuria is mainly attributed to impairment of the glomerular filtration\nbarrier and reabsorption dysfunction of tubular epithelial cells. Mesangial matrix expansion is common\nin both IgAN and DKD. In IgAN, this phenomenon is mainly driven by the deposition of Gd-IgA1 in the\nmesangium [16], whereas in DKD, it’s initiated with hyperglycaemic stimulation [17]. Mesangial matrix\nexpansion squeezes capillary loops, destroying glomerular filtration barrier and reducing blood flow,\neventually resulting in glomerulosclerosis [18]. GBM thickening results from the accumulation of advanced\nglycosylation end products, the activation of the inflammatory signalling pathway, and oxidative stress\ndamage, which destroy normal structure of filtration membrane [19,20]. Podocytes and their foot processes form a critical component of the glomerular filtration barrier and are essential to maintain renal\nfiltration selectivity and prevent protein leakage [21]. Fusion or even disappearance of foot processes can\nlead to proteinuria [22]. In DKD, impaired glycolysis enhances ornithine decomposition, and then disturb\ncytoskeletal remodelling, resulting in foot process fusion [23]. Mesangial matrix expansion, GBM thickening and foot process fusion are positively correlated with proteinuria level [24–27]. Tubular epithelial cells are\nresponsible for reabsorbing most of the small-molecule proteins filtered by the glomerulus. When the\nrenal tubule atrophied, the tight junctions of tubule epithelial cells were destroyed and transformed into\na fibrotic phenotype. The reabsorption function decreases, the small molecular protein in urine increase\n[28]. In DKD, the GBM thickens progressively under\nhyperglycaemia but does not rupture, thereby preventing extravasation of red blood cells. The assembled Gd-IgA1-containing immune complexes trigger complement activation\npredominantly via the lectin and alternative pathways, driving the sequential formation of C3 convertase\nand membrane attack complex. This complement cascade promotes mesangial cell proliferation, podocyte injury and contributes to the progressive renal damage [9].
