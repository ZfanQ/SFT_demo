This study examined hirsutine's protective effects against high glucose-induced injury in MPC5 podocytes, showing improved viability and reduced oxidative stress and apoptosis. However, the findings are based on an in vitro model without direct autophagic flux analysis or genetic confirmation of NRF2 involvement. The results suggest hirsutine may target apoptosis, autophagy, and ferroptosis, but further in vivo research is required to validate its therapeutic potential for diabetic nephropathy.
