# Project Working Rules

## Publication destinations

- Use `ZfanQ/SFT_demo` for test code, prompts, pilot results, diagnostics and validation work.
- Further publication to `m2b3/Summarize_LLM_SFT` waits for credibility review and the user's confirmation. Do not automatically mirror experimental changes to that repository.
- Existing supervisor-facing experimental reports are historical records with stated limitations. Do not delete them or rewrite their history merely to apply the new destination policy.
- See `docs/PUBLISHING.md` for experiment status and the review process.

## Research records

- Write supervisor-facing reports in English.
- Preserve original papers, summaries, previous results and teammate work. Version changed inputs, rubrics, extraction or model configurations.
- Keep summary factuality, abstract relevance, ranking-reference agreement and performance claims distinct.
- Describe GPT findings as automated judgments until independently assessed. Structural validity, quote presence and score agreement alone do not certify correctness.
- Keep secrets, model weights and local runtime caches out of publication changes. Commit only the intended files.
